# Incremental Updates And Reusable Knowledge

Use for requested refreshes or reuse of existing research. Read the prior scope, as-of boundary, report, claims, source snapshots, unresolved questions and update triggers. Preserve old observations and conclusions; update means a dated delta, not rewriting history or repeating every search.

## Compare Before Calling Something A Conflict

Compare subject, metric/construct, units, population, horizon, conditions and measurement method. Classify pairs as agreement, comparable contradiction, different claim, outdated applicability or unresolved identity. Unknown is neither agreement nor contradiction. A newer timestamp alone does not settle a dispute; changed populations or editions can explain a difference. Preserve both supports and the rationale for a revised conclusion.

Recheck changed entities/entrants, material numbers and vintages, thesis milestones, corrections/retractions and new counterevidence. Propagate updates only through dependent claims, sections and decisions. Report verified-no-change when that is what the check supports. Failure to access a source does not show that nothing changed.

## Reuse Without A Second Truth Store

Reuse the project's authorized research index or knowledge system, not a new global home-directory wiki. Stable source identity can use a canonical URL/DOI plus version; do not merge distinct editions/datasets by aggressively deleting URL parameters. Claim identity is the run and local ID, with a dated version when scope changes; semantic similarity does not authorize merging statements. Links between claims record agreement/conflict/different-scope/unknown with evidence on both sides.

A source assessment is scoped to a claim, date and use. Reuse provenance, but re-evaluate authority, corrections and freshness when relevant; do not retain a historical maximum credibility score as permanent truth. A rejected recommendation can still leave valuable evidence. Conversely a user accepting a recommendation does not certify the evidence.

Atomic findings worth reuse retain the assertion, conditions, supporting and opposing sources, inference, uncertainty, observed date and reopening trigger. Save them only when there is a reuse need; the claim ledger already serves a single report. Do not promote a one-off finding into general agent instructions or training data without the separate flywheel/migration checks.

## Deliver The Delta

State what changed, what remains supported, which conclusions/actions are affected and what remains unknown. Link old and new versions and their evidence. A lean report can retain its comparison/history in the existing version system and appendix; a refresh alone does not require migration to an audit package. For the native package, use a new dated directory/revision so old receipts remain attributable; revalidate affected sources, arithmetic, claims, summary and report. Do not reuse a semantic verification receipt after any of its inputs changed.

Keep future refresh targets concrete: entity/metric/claim, source and locator, as-of date, event that would matter, dependent sections and outstanding gap. A suggested trigger is not permission to schedule monitoring. Save an actual application/decision outcome only if the user supplied it or authorized tracking; silence is not consent or a deferred decision.
