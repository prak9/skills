# Value Transmission And Incidence

Use this contract when technology progress, national development, industrial policy, real estate, household wealth, capital flows or another macro narrative is used to support a company or stock conclusion. It is a bridge into company underwriting, not a standalone macro template. Skip it for narrow arithmetic and business questions that do not rely on such a narrative.

## Keep The Layers Separate

Locate each claim before linking it:

1. **Product or technical capability:** what becomes possible, reliable and commercially usable?
2. **Policy or national objective:** what outcome is being optimized and which constraint is relaxed?
3. **Households and other stakeholders:** whose income, balance sheet, purchasing power or risk changes?
4. **Industry:** how do demand, capacity, price and competition respond?
5. **Company:** what reaches revenue, unit economics, cash needs and resilience?
6. **Common equity:** what remains after labor, suppliers, taxes, debt, required reinvestment, minority interests and dilution?
7. **Investor:** what return remains after the purchase price, time, distributions, taxes/costs and exit assumptions, measured in the relevant liability currency and real purchasing power when material?

Do not skip a layer merely because the preceding claim is important or well supported. AI capability does not establish a vendor's margins; manufacturing strength does not establish household wealth; industry growth does not establish per-share growth; company success does not establish an attractive stock at today's price.

## Trace Rights To Per-Share Payoff

Start with the security and legal/economic claim actually owned. Then trace:

```text
change or capability
-> payer/customer behavior and realized price/volume
-> company revenue and contribution economics
-> working capital, maintenance and growth investment
-> financing, senior claims, taxes and minority leakage
-> cash/value attributable to common equity
-> net shares and per-share outcome
-> return from the current price over a stated horizon
```

At each arrow name the mechanism, timing, offset, strongest rival and observable evidence. If a necessary link is unavailable, stop the conclusion at the last supported layer. Do not rescue the chain with TAM, policy importance, revenue scale or a valuation multiple.

For growth, compare incremental economics rather than only scale. Revenue or production can rise while margins, cash generation, return on new capital or per-share value falls. Reconcile capacity additions, price pressure, subsidies, working capital, debt, dilution and capital allocation. State who can capture the incremental surplus and for how long.

## Distinguish Policy Target, Protection And Cost

For a material policy claim, identify:

- the actual objective and success metric;
- the protected beneficiary or right—consumer access, delivery, employment, financial stability, strategic capacity, company solvency or common shareholders;
- the instrument and transmission path;
- who pays through lower price, margin, return, taxes, dilution or restricted choice;
- timing, implementation uncertainty and plausible competing objectives.

Support can improve one constraint while imposing another. A strategically important industry may gain financing and lose pricing power; protection of customers or the financial system need not protect incumbent equity. Treat “target” and “cost” as questions that can coexist, not permanent labels. Policy importance is neither a bailout promise nor a valuation premium.

Economic interdependence raises the cost of conflict but does not rule it out. For geopolitical claims, map the company's exposed markets, critical inputs, legal rights and realistic substitutes; then distinguish effects on volume, cost, capital access and asset ownership. Do not turn a costly policy outcome into an impossible one.

## Audit Stocks, Flows And Causality

Classify every load-bearing macro number:

- **stock:** assets, debt, deposits, installed capacity at a date;
- **flow:** income, consumption, investment, credit creation over a period;
- **transaction value:** sales or turnover during a period;
- **revaluation:** a price change applied to an existing asset;
- **capital formation or financing:** spending and the source/claim that funds it.

Do not net or equate unlike objects. A fall in property value does not release equivalent cash into factories; lower home sales do not prove the same amount financed manufacturing. A simultaneous decline and increase suggests a research question, not a traced transfer. Establish the funding sources, intermediaries and counterfactual before making a causal allocation claim.

Separate accounting from behavior. For a household with housing value `H`, other net assets `F`, debt `D` and net worth `W = H + F - D`, a housing-price decline `d` produces an illustrative net-worth loss ratio `dH / W` if the other terms stay fixed. This shows leverage, not a population estimate. National conclusions require the distributions of ownership, leverage, purchase timing, geography, age and renter/non-owner exposure. Keep household net worth, monthly cash flow and consumption response distinct.

Deposit growth can coexist with lower total wealth, deleveraging and weak risk appetite. In a secondary-market stock purchase, the buyer's deposit normally becomes the seller's deposit; system-wide deposits do not mechanically disappear into equities. Claims about “cash on the sidelines” need evidence about holders, issuance/fund flows, risk tolerance, valuation and the behavior that changes equilibrium prices.

## Translate The Narrative Into Underwriting

Use a compact layer table or equivalent prose only for material links:

| Layer | Supported observation | Mechanism to next layer | Leakage / rival | Investment consequence | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then answer four questions:

1. What right does the security confer, and what cash or control can actually reach it?
2. Which party benefits from the underlying change, and which party bears the cost?
3. Which company economics and per-share variables must improve for the narrative to matter?
4. What current-price requirement and future observation would confirm or break the case?

When the conclusion depends on a broad average, inspect distribution and aggregation. When it depends on a historical analogy or famous investor's statement, preserve the speaker's claim but independently verify numbers, definitions, causal steps and current applicability. An insightful mechanism can survive while its headline magnitude remains unverified.

## Finish At The Supported Layer

Valid conclusions include: national or customer benefit without proven shareholder benefit; company improvement without attractive prospective return; lower price with the operating engine intact but valuation still unresolved; policy support with uncertain incidence; or no demonstrated transmission edge. Do not force a stock verdict from a macro observation.

For investment work, carry supported links into coherent operating scenarios, capital requirements, dilution, valuation and falsifiers. A margin of safety reflects both discount and estimation reliability: a narrow value range may make a moderate discount meaningful, while a wide or discontinuous range can leave the same apparent discount uninformative. Spreadsheet precision cannot compensate for an unbounded critical mechanism; if feasible research still cannot bound it, stop at `Too Hard at present` and name the evidence that could reopen the case.
