# Uncertainty, Marginal Information, And Exposure

Use for opportunity/risk, price, allocation comparisons, sizing, portfolio fit, or the effect of new information. Answer only the requested decision; single-company research does not authorize a portfolio review, watchlist, trade, alert or external record.

## Separate Belief, Price And Commitment

Keep three judgments distinct: **thesis quality** (supported mechanism and falsifier), **price attractiveness** (forward net payoffs from today's quote), and **exposure** (estimation error, tail loss, correlation, liquidity and opportunity cost). A better business can offer a worse return after its price rises. A small position does not validate a weak thesis, and concentrated research need not mean concentrated capital.

Price attractiveness is a relation, not a company attribute. State both sides on the same security, currency and date: the evidence-supported per-share payoff/value range and the current price plus its implied operating requirements. Then show the horizon, distributions, dilution, realization route and loss under the strongest credible failure case. Historical cost, distance from a prior high, a low multiple or a target above the quote cannot supply the missing side of that comparison.

A margin of safety depends on both the price discount and the reliability of the value range. A quote below a precise-looking point estimate offers little protection when the plausible range is wide, key rights are uncertain or downside is discontinuous. Show which uncertainty the discount can absorb and which thesis failure it cannot; do not use a low multiple or a large drawdown as a substitute for bounded value.

Use conditional outcomes rather than a lone target. A distribution needs defensible probabilities; otherwise give scenarios and sensitivities, not invented odds. Respect an explicitly chosen strategy as a constraint, not a universal market law.

An option-implied distribution is an observable pricing distribution, not a shortcut around that requirement. Label its expiry, extraction time, spot/forward basis, rate, contract style, liquidity screen and method. Risk-neutral probabilities incorporate risk premia and hedging pressure and therefore must not be inserted as objective bull/base/bear probabilities or used alone for sizing. Use [the dedicated method](options-implied-distribution.md) to expose threshold/range probabilities and data-quality limits.

## Update Only What The Evidence Changes

Define the event, population, horizon and resolution rule before assigning a probability. Ground priors in applicable base rates and company economics, retaining comparability limits. Use ranges when evidence is weak; unsupported likelihoods do not become precise posteriors. Coexisting mechanisms are not mutually exclusive scenarios and need not sum to one.

Trace `observed signals -> shared cause -> rivals -> incremental evidence`. Earnings, guidance, supplier orders and analyst revisions originating from one event are not independent confirmations. Distinguish incremental, overlapping, redundant and contradictory evidence; preserve the previous estimate and explain which probability, payoff or feasibility assumption changes. If no incremental evidence warrants a belief update, leaving it unchanged is valid. Separate that from a price-only return update. Position size, familiarity, research hours, CEO access, insider purchases and recent price moves earn no automatic confidence increase.

Use [sequential Bayesian updates](mode-b-bayesian-growth.md#sequential-bayesian-updates) to carry the latest posterior forward as the next prior, compare conditional evidence under rival explanations, and propagate any warranted revision into the current bet decision. Reuse the existing dated thesis record rather than recounting evidence or starting a parallel ledger.

For repeated forecasts, retain the dated information set, event definition, horizon, resolution rule and outcome. Evaluate calibration and discrimination only on comparable resolved forecasts, preserving not-yet-due and unresolvable cases; one-off probability bands are not objective odds. For stochastic valuation or repeated scoring, check whether realized frequencies and ranked outcomes are consistent with the stated probabilities, using strata or reference classes only when the sample supports them; deterministic DCFs do not require this machinery. Distinguish sampling error, parameter uncertainty and structural error: more simulation draws cannot validate assumed distributions. Preserve input dependencies; use hypothetical sensitivity when distributions lack support.

## Compare Forward Payoffs, Not Attention

Use coherent scenario returns including financing/dilution, relevant taxes, fees, cash distributions and exit/terminal assumptions. Where probabilities are supported:

`Net EV = sum(scenario probability * scenario net return)`

Show the dominant downside, holding period, interim funding/liquidity path and estimation sensitivity alongside the mean. A positive average cannot override a stated survival constraint. Price is information, not a unique probability distribution: compare independently evidenced business estimates with conditional price requirements, without counting news already embedded in a prior twice.

For an allocation request, compare eligible holdings and watchlist candidates at common as-of dates, horizons, currencies and net-return definitions using **current prices**, not purchase costs. Retain differences in evidence freshness and uncertainty. A compact comparison needs supported payoffs, probability assumptions if defensible, funding/dilution, liquidity and incremental portfolio risk—not equal precision for every name.

An upside/downside ratio compares specified magnitudes, not probabilities, complete tails or expected return. Identify its scenarios and horizon; near-zero modeled downside makes it unstable, not risk-free. Do not rank on it alone. If estimates overlap or decisive inputs are unavailable, give a conditional or unresolved ranking, not a fabricated allocation.

Distinguish **new cash, retention, adding, switching and cash**. A switch must beat retaining the holding and relevant feasible alternatives on net wealth after sale taxes, fees and other costs reduce redeployable capital. A slightly higher gross return need not justify switching. Conversely, “I would not add today” does not alone imply “sell everything”: costs, risk and available alternatives can make the gates asymmetric. Missing tax/portfolio inputs limits allocation advice, not standalone research.

## Required Final Bet Recommendation

For company-level investment-value or valuation work, finish after the scenario/valuation and observation synthesis with **whether the offered bet merits capital at the dated price**, not merely whether the business is good. This is conditional research guidance, not order execution. Business-only work, raw extraction and explicitly narrow arithmetic do not inherit this section.

State **conditional commitment, wait, or decline**, with the reason. If a holding is actually supplied, distinguish retention, adding, switching, reduction and exit; never assume an existing position. A sizing gap need not prevent a supported price/edge judgment. A probability or decisive economic gap must remain visible rather than being filled to produce a recommendation.

Make the decisive chain auditable: **original evidence/date → business hypothesis and strongest rival → basis/range for probabilities → scenario owner cash and per-share payoff → calculation → binding uncertainty/risk condition → action and update trigger**. Explain why the estimate differs from the price's conditional requirements and what would erase that difference. An unsupported probability repeated by several sources is not underwriting evidence. Reuse the same scenario model; do not create a separate betting model with more favorable inputs.

### Compute The Offered Edge

- Fix security, currency, quote date and horizon. Let `I0` be the actual initial outlay, including entry costs, and `W_s,T` the scenario's net terminal wealth. Reconcile distributions, dilution, taxes/fees and any reinvestment/timing convention before computing `r_s = W_s,T/I0 − 1`. If cash-flow timing matters, show dated cash flows and distinguish holding-period return, present value and IRR; do not count dividends twice or turn a discounted present value into an undiscounted exit price.
- With defensible mutually exclusive probabilities, show `E[r] = Σ p_s r_s` and, where a relevant supported hurdle/reference is available, `E[excess] = Σ p_s (r_s − r_ref,s)` on the same net basis and horizon. Missing reference inputs are a gap, not permission to invent a risk-free rate or personal hurdle. Report loss probability `Σ p_s for r_s < 0`, consequential tail loss and interim liquidity/funding risk alongside the mean. Expected wealth is not the outcome an investor is most likely to realize; a nonlinear annualization of the mean is not the mean of scenario CAGRs or geometric growth.
- For a genuinely two-state net bet with profit `G > 0`, loss magnitude `L > 0` and win probability `p`: `EV = pG − (1−p)L`; break-even `p_BE = L/(G+L)`; net payoff odds `G/L` (decimal payout odds `1+G/L`). Against a required net monetary gain `H` over the same horizon, `p_BE,H = (L+H)/(G+L)`; a threshold above 1 is unattainable, while one at or below 0 is met by every win probability under these fixed payoffs. Show substituted inputs and results. These are **conditional break-even probabilities**, not independently observed market beliefs.
- Do not collapse a multi-state stock into bull-versus-bear odds while dropping the base case, financing failure or other material states. One stock quote does not identify real-world probabilities; option-implied risk-neutral probabilities remain separate. When probabilities are unidentifiable, show `EV(p)`/joint sensitivities and the decision-switch threshold, explicitly conditional on the held-fixed payoffs, rather than a manufactured point posterior.

The user's uploaded book example illustrates the arithmetic, not a verified historical forecast: at assumed `p=25%`, net profit `$520,000` and loss `$80,000`, `EV=.25×520,000−.75×80,000=$70,000`; `p_BE=80,000/600,000=13.33%`; return on an `$80,000` stake is conditionally `87.5%`, with `75%` probability of losing that stake. The displayed 13% is rounded, not an exact second probability. Neither the probability nor the profit margin transfers to a stock. A positive one-shot expectation does not promise that losses will be recovered by repetition.

### Turn Edge Into A Bounded Recommendation

Test whether the advantage survives plausible **joint** deterioration in probabilities, realized prices/margins, delays, funding/dilution, terminal value and costs. State the observation or price/probability threshold that changes the action. Do not impose an arbitrary universal edge cutoff or claim robustness from varying one convenient parameter. If the sign changes under credible estimation error, call the opportunity fragile or unresolved and explain what evidence or terms would make it actionable. Positive expected excess is necessary under the stated expectation-based criterion, not sufficient compensation for every risk or preference.

Separate **edge demonstrated**, **risk acceptable**, and **size identifiable**. A positive EV can still fail a funding, permanent-loss, concentration or liquidity constraint; a high win rate can still hide a negative EV. With missing portfolio or loss-budget inputs, give the conditional posture and a per-unit-capital illustration, not a nominal “starter” percentage, assumed wealth or full-Kelly instruction. There is no mandatory real-money trial.

When actual sizing inputs are supplied, show the controlling constraint and arithmetic. For a stated joint stress, if `B` is allowed portfolio loss as a fraction of NAV, `L_other` the loss of other exposures on that same NAV basis excluding this issuer, and `d > 0` this issuer's stressed fractional loss, a necessary loss-budget cap is `w_loss = max(0, (B−L_other)/d)`. Cap **total current-market-value weight** further by concentration, executable liquidity and funding limits. Maximum incremental capacity is `max(0, w_cap−w_current)`; it is an upper bound, **not a recommended target**. A stress loss is not a guaranteed downside floor; test credible additional tails. If other exposures already exhaust the budget, zero new capacity does not solve the existing portfolio breach. Unknown limits remain unknown, and this linear scenario constraint is not a portfolio optimizer.

End in plain language: **why commit or not now; which evidence supports the edge; the calculation and its limits; feasible commitment or unresolved sizing constraint; and what changes the answer**. Price-only changes may alter the offered return without changing the business probabilities. No edge demonstrated, or a good business at poor terms, can rationally end in no bet.

## Let Evidence Earn Commitment, Not Exemptions

Before calling a position high-conviction or considering concentration, run an adversarial underwriting gate. Seek the strongest accessible, informed opposing thesis—such as serious bear research, a domain expert's contrary mechanism or a credible short case—rather than the loudest critic or a token devil's advocate. Decompose it into factual premises, causal links, timing and valuation consequences; trace each premise to original evidence and record whether it is refuted, supported or still unresolved. Revalue the scenarios under the strongest surviving version. A material unresolved bear claim caps warranted confidence and exposure even when the base case remains attractive; absence of a published short case proves nothing. Contacting a short seller or expert is neither required nor authorized by research alone.

Small-to-core progression is optional, not a prescribed number of holdings or starting percentage. Operating execution, cash conversion, financing resilience and management conduct can strengthen a case; price appreciation and elapsed time alone cannot. Research and a watchlist can mature a thesis without a real-money starter position. Success-selected wealthy investors do not establish an ex-ante selection edge.

Apply the same fresh-capital test to averaging up and down: update business/payoffs, current price, alternatives and incremental risk. A falling price proves neither cheapness nor failure; a rising price proves neither strength nor overvaluation. Deteriorating economics, debt/refinancing, cash runway, dilution or obsolescence can make averaging down unsafe; a “winner” label is no exemption. Losses or leverage are not universal bans unless the user adopted them. Larger scale does not by itself justify a higher multiple: test diversification, incremental returns, capital needs and durability.

Assess **all existing exposure at current market value**, including positions enlarged by appreciation. “It earned its weight” does not waive concentration limits. Relate stressed holding losses to portfolio loss, common exposures and executable exit capacity; conviction can rise while permissible exposure falls. Changed personal liquidity needs or portfolio scale can also change feasibility without changing the company.

Do not derive a position percentage without sufficient portfolio/exposure, loss-budget, liquidity, tax/financing and payoff inputs. Give a conditional posture and binding gate instead. Kelly is at most a diagnostic upper bound for sufficiently repeatable, estimable bets with bounded losses and modeled dependence, never a universal `mu / variance` recommendation. Shrink uncertain edge estimates and cap model-derived weights by stressed-loss, concentration/factor and executable-liquidity constraints, all expressed on the same portfolio-weight basis. Explain the binding constraint; unknown caps do not justify a guessed percentage.

Where holdings are supplied, inspect shared market, duration, size, rates, FX, commodity, geography, sector and thesis-specific exposures as relevant. Ticker count is not independent risk count. Stress correlated losses during deleveraging and illiquidity; with missing holdings, mark the portfolio conclusion unverified.

Match the thesis clock to the capital and mandate clock. A sound long-duration case can still be uninvestable for a vehicle facing near-term redemptions, leverage, benchmark limits or other forced-liquidity conditions. Conversely, long-locked capital does not make a weak thesis correct. State the operating evidence window, plausible repricing window and earliest forced-liquidity date separately. A strategy that requires patient ownership needs funding and liquidity terms capable of surviving its expected drawdowns and periods of relative underperformance.

## Define Waiting And Exit Conditions

Entry and exit gates depend on evidence, feasibility, net returns and risk constraints, not universal PE discounts. Discount to uncertain future value must be translated into horizon return and distributions, not guaranteed margin of safety. An unchanged business may warrant a valuation-only update when price crosses a return gate.

Separate waiting for **operating delivery**, **evidence/disclosure**, and **market repricing**. Include binding prerequisites such as funding, permits or delivery capability; a better price cannot satisfy an unmet evidence or feasibility gate. Flat prices alone do not falsify an on-track thesis or prove cheapness; repeated missed operating milestones and dwindling funding are not cured by patience or a fixed three-year horizon. Name the next discriminating result, economically justified window and reopening/invalidation condition. At expiry distinguish delay, failure and unresolvable observation; neither silently extend the forecast nor force a failure. Complete accessible checks now. Preserve old claims and assess new business cases separately using [thesis reconciliation](thesis-reconciliation.md).

A conditional reduction/exit can follow thesis deterioration, trust/governance evidence, valuation, a materially better net alternative, or a portfolio constraint. Not every sale requires a damaged company, and not every price drop requires a sale. State the supported reason, uncertainty and execution limits; thin liquidity can prevent an immediate full exit. Feelings of betrayal or boredom are prompts to investigate, not sufficient proof.

Treat regime changes and edge decay as diagnoses, not automatic switches. Examine estimate revisions, valuation convergence, mechanism changes, crowding/capacity and costs as relevant; do not require a guessed numerical half-life or treat an unvalidated classifier as an oracle. Preserve uncertainty about structural change, measurement failure and execution effects.

## Deliver The Decision-Relevant Difference

Lead with what changed—or why no change is warranted—then the evidence, forward payoff/tail, binding constraint and next observation relevant to the request. Include quantitative probabilities, portfolio factors or sizing only when supported and needed. Do not expand a narrow comparison into a complete allocation report.
