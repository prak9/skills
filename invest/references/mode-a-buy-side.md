# Buy-Side Company Research

Use for business understanding, a bare ticker, stock analysis, an IC memo, re-underwriting or thesis updates. The entrypoint owns source discipline, required final scenarios, writing and delivery. This reference adds research depth and analytical coverage—not another report template.

## Depth And Reading

- **Standard, default:** read [business quality](business-quality.md), establish the recent-four-period baseline, and examine the perspectives below. Stock-opportunity/valuation work also reads [joint scenarios](mode-b-bayesian-growth.md).
- **Quick:** only for a brief/quick request. Compress the decisive evidence and reasoning, retaining the four-period baseline for new underwriting and the required scenario conclusion when applicable.
- **Full:** deep/comprehensive research, IC memos, re-underwriting, long history or substantive DCF/normalized EPS/reverse-DCF reconstruction reads [longitudinal underwriting](underwriting-loop.md). Retain its longer history, recent-three-month external research discovery, financial model, allocation/financing bridges, assumption support, valuation attribution and consistency audit. Do not silently substitute a toy model or Quick report; disclose unavailable components.
- **Monitoring:** read [thesis reconciliation](thesis-reconciliation.md), update affected prior claims and reuse valid history. No material change warrants a concise finding, not a fresh report.

Business-only work can end with supported economics and unresolved questions. Narrow supplied-input arithmetic follows its direct route.

## Company Snapshot And Calculation Trail

Borrow the compact, comparable company-snapshot idea associated with Value Line, not its ratings, marketing language or a subscription dependency. After the research answer, let a reader quickly identify the business's earnings engine, financial condition and price hurdle. Use original disclosures for material facts; a commercial summary is a lead or cross-check, not a replacement for source verification.

Select the metrics that explain this company. Normally cover revenue and growth, profitability, cash conversion, cash/debt and share count, capital efficiency, and current valuation when in scope; add the decisive business unit or cohort metric. Show comparable annual history (often three years when available) plus the latest meaningful reported/TTM period. Preserve actual reporting cadence and missing data; do not invent quarters or annualize a seasonal quarter. A short supplied-data analysis uses its available periods rather than fabricating history.

For comparable additive flows, TTM may be derived as latest FY + current YTD − prior-year matching YTD; rebuild EPS and ratios from appropriate inputs instead of applying that identity mechanically. Monitoring reuses the verified snapshot and updates affected rows rather than reconstructing unchanged history.

The snapshot may use `metric/basis | current and comparable history | meaning/source`. Each material derived value also needs a trace: **definition/formula → substituted sourced inputs → arithmetic/result → investment implication and limit**. Put decisive calculations beside the snapshot or claim; longer reconciliations can use linked appendix rows. Define a formula once across comparable years, but retain the inputs for each result. A spreadsheet link, unexplained score or bare percentage is not the calculation process.

Keep the lead board compact and put the full calculation trail below it or in an appendix. Do not list every computable multiple or per-share restatement merely because inputs exist; retain alternative representations when requested or when their basis changes the judgment. Shared units, sources and proxy caveats can live in table notes, with material local qualifications beside the affected value.

| Economic question | Typical metrics and calculation |
|---|---|
| Scale and direction | Revenue growth = current/prior − 1; CAGR = (end/start)^(1/years) − 1 when positive, comparable endpoints support it. Separate volume, realized price/mix, acquisitions and FX where material. |
| Profit retained | Gross/operating/net margin = the matching profit/revenue. Distinguish EBIT, pretax profit, consolidated net profit and profit attributable to common shareholders. |
| Profit becomes cash | Show CFO, cash capex and `CFO − cash capex` as a labeled FCF proxy; CFO/net income requires matching scope and becomes uninformative around zero or losses. Reconcile maintenance, growth investment, leases, financing and SBC before calling it sustainable owner cash. |
| Capital efficiency | Explicitly report ROIC = NOPAT/average operating invested capital, with EBIT → operating tax → NOPAT and opening/closing capital → average capital shown. Distinguish disclosed-basis proxies from normalized estimates. ROE = common-attributable net profit/average common equity is a different measure, not a substitute for a calculable non-financial-company ROIC. |
| Financial survival | Show gross debt, maturities, unrestricted/restricted cash and necessary operating cash; define the cash deducted in net debt and the claims included. Book equity or inventory is not a liquidation floor. |
| Shareholder claim and price | Market cap = dated price × corresponding current shares. Historical diluted EPS uses period weighted-average diluted shares; forward per-share values use the supported future diluted count. Reconcile ADS/share-class and FX bases rather than interchanging these counts. |
| Valuation and cash yield | P/E = price/matching EPS; P/B = corresponding equity market value/common book equity. EV = equity value + included senior/minority claims − qualifying nonoperating assets/cash; specify lease and cash treatment. Use enterprise earnings/cash with EV and common-equity earnings/cash with equity price; explain reported versus economic adjustments. |
| What the current price permits | Show conditional operating requirements, per-share values and return arithmetic. Discount to value = (V − P)/V; upside from price = V/P − 1. These have different denominators and neither proves safety when V is unreliable. |

For every load-bearing ratio, make the numerator, denominator, tax treatment, date/period and source visible. Negative or unstable denominators may make a ratio `不适用`; unavailable inputs remain `未核验`, not zero. State assumptions separately from disclosed facts and preserve precision until final rounding.

For substantive non-financial company research, the delivered snapshot includes the latest meaningful annual/TTM **ROIC and comparable history where reconstructible**, even when the user did not name the metric. Follow [capital-return measurement](business-quality.md#measure-roic-and-new-investment-returns) for scope and adjustments. An issuer not publishing ROIC is not a reason to skip reconstructible statement inputs. If a material input remains unavailable after feasible checks, retain a visible `ROIC 未核验` or `不适用` row, the precise missing input/basis, any supported conditional range and its consequence for the judgment; do not insert a guessed rate.

When expansion or heavy investment carries the thesis, separate **group historical ROIC** from **incremental/project returns**. Deliver what each can establish and what remains unresolved. Missing AI/segment/cohort capital or future cash flows does not excuse omitting a calculable group return; neither a segment EBITA margin nor a management payback claim fills either gap. Explain whether supported returns exceed the relevant capital cost and whether the forecast requires better incremental returns than the evidence supports. Narrow calculations and price-only monitoring retain scope; banks/insurers use appropriate equity/regulatory-capital measures rather than an industrial ROIC.

Adapt the board rather than force an industrial template: banks/insurers may need book capital, credit/underwriting, funding and regulatory metrics; retailers need store/inventory economics; software needs retention and acquisition/service economics. The snapshot should help answer **how it earns, why the spread persists, how much cash can reach each share, and whether the price compensates for error**. It does not replace the countercase, causal sandbox or final scenario distribution.

## Broad Perspectives, One Judgment

For Standard/Full, proactively inspect each relevant perspective before narrowing to the decisive debates. These are distinct questions, not separate language-model calls, votes or compulsory sections.

| Perspective | What must be learned |
|---|---|
| Rights and value capture | Which security is owned, who pays, who retains the benefit, and what reaches common shareholders after senior claims, governance and dilution. Read [value transmission](value-transmission.md) when macro/policy/technology carries the case. |
| Earnings engine | How price, paid volume, mix, delivery cost, necessary support and capital create normalized owner cash; why the spread exists, persists or erodes. |
| Competition and runway | Obtainable **profitable** demand, penetration, substitution, incumbent/challenger response, incremental capital returns and limits to growth duration. Analyze these in business/scenario economics; adjusted PEG is not required. |
| Financial architecture and management | Cash conversion, maintenance versus growth spending, working capital, leverage, commitments, incentives, distributions and narrative-versus-footnote discrepancies. |
| Competing paths and expectations | Independently supported future operating paths versus dated observed consensus and conditional current-price requirements. [B](mode-b-bayesian-growth.md) supplies the causal sandbox and probability/value synthesis. |
| Material changes | Discover product, procurement, supply, pricing, cost and regulatory changes, not just pasted news. Read [D](mode-d-serenity-alpha.md) when transmission is consequential. |
| Downside and survival | Strongest coherent countercase; joint failure, funding, dilution and liquidity. Use [uncertainty and exposure](uncertainty-exposure.md), especially before discussing concentration. |
| Price behavior and market expectations | Separate earnings revisions from multiple/liquidity/holder effects. Attempt the [one-year options distribution](options-implied-distribution.md) under the entrypoint's data gates; keep it risk-neutral. |

Each lens should add a supported inference, challenged assumption or material unknown. Data gaps limit calculations, not feasible qualitative inquiry. Integrate disagreements and shared dependencies rather than average incompatible model outputs.

## Establish The Operating Sequence

For new underwriting inspect the latest four reported quarters and their earnings materials/calls, including available Q&A. Q4 and its annual filing are one economic period. Use the issuer's actual cadence for semiannual/irregular reporting, compare equal-duration periods and label missing calls or quarters. This is distinct from Full's recent-three-month external research window.

Track metrics that change judgment: economic drivers, contemporaneous guidance/consensus versus actuals, revisions, promised versus delivered milestones and revealing Q&A. Contrast management narrative with cash conversion, accounting policies, segment notes, commitments and contingencies. An unanswered question is neither confirmation nor failure; a footnote discrepancy is an investigation target, not proof of misconduct. Check seasonality, mix, acquisitions, accounting and disclosure lags before naming a turning point.

## Form And Challenge The Thesis

Fix security, exchange, fiscal year, currency, cutoff, current price and horizon. Frame a provisional hypothesis and the few debates capable of changing value; do not select the final verdict before the causal sandbox. The report may lead with the **result after research**, not pretend that the opening judgment was the starting evidence.

Trace a representative collected transaction from customer need through willingness to pay and revenue recognition to owner cash. Reconstruct segment economics; distinguish demand from financing, accounting or underinvestment effects. Investigate the weakest capture, durability, funding or per-share link with original footnotes/contracts/call passages and relevant customer, supplier or competitor evidence. The [business-quality reference](business-quality.md) supplies deeper financial architecture, moat formation and incumbent-advantage checks.

Write the strongest coherent countercase and its differential prediction, without a theatrical persona or fixed word count. If it better explains the evidence, change the conclusion to impairment, unattractive, no demonstrated edge or unresolved. A weak long case is not automatically a short: borrow, financing, timing, loss asymmetry and an independent short payoff still matter.

A claimed variant compares an observed dated expectation or explicit price-implied condition with an independently supported view. Do not invent consensus. Identify the evidenced mispricing mechanism—forced ownership change, accounting lag, category contamination, horizon mismatch—when relevant. Price decline, public good news, national competitiveness or low coverage alone proves none of these.

After an earnings miss or selloff, separate changes in the customer/economic engine, reporting timing/magnitude, and price/holder/liquidity response. Reconcile the event with the earlier supported mechanism before calling it noise. Historical scale and a large decline do not prove the engine intact.

## Value Against The Price

Apply [B's causal sandbox](mode-b-bayesian-growth.md#causal-sandbox-before-scenario-judgment) before final probabilities or verdict. Carry capacity, rival responses, capital, working capital, financing and dilution through coherent future paths, then value each and compare with price requirements. Do not fit the operating forecast to a desired target.

Choose DCF, normalized earnings, SOTP, NAV or multiples by economic fit. Full triangulates when informative; agreement between forward and reverse models sharing assumptions is not independent confirmation. Reconcile per-share rights, diluted shares, net debt/senior claims, distributions and currency. Distinguish present-value gaps from dated holding-period returns.

One price identifies neither a unique forecast nor probability distribution. Keep observed consensus, conditional reverse-valuation solutions and independent underwriting separate. Label sensitive conclusions, disclose missing inputs and retain estimation error. Neither fixed PE bands nor positive FCF, founder ownership or revenue acceleration are universal investment laws. Assess investment-phase losses and cyclical normalization on their economics.

## Delivery And Readiness

Lead the finished report with the supported answer, return source and live debate. Explain the business mechanism, decisive evidence, strongest countercase and uncertainty in the order needed for understanding, then close with the entrypoint's scenarios, valuation, signals and six-month catalysts. Full also applies [financial evidence and claim audit](financial-evidence.md); propagate corrections to affected conclusions. Preserve the [writing handoff](../SKILL.md#required-writing-pass-for-final-articles).

Research may rationally end in waiting. Record the business conditions, valuation hurdle and invalidation evidence that would reopen the case; a reached price threshold does not override deteriorating economics. Distinguish strategy exclusion, unattractive price, no demonstrated edge and insufficient understanding. Use `Too Hard at present` only for a decisive link still unbounded after feasible inquiry, with the resolving question and evidence. Do not confuse time spent researching with conviction earned.
