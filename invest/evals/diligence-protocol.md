## Execution

Freeze the skill snapshot, packet and criteria before candidate edits. Give the executor only each ID and prompt, the selected skill entry and routed resources, not criteria, expected behavior, numeric checks, a diff or prior findings. Inputs are self-contained hypotheticals; no live stock search, external mutation or invented source is needed. Local arithmetic is allowed. Keep model/effort, tool access and inputs fixed; record unavailable metadata as unknown, not an invented version.

Run cases in fresh contexts for controlled comparisons. A same-context batch is only a forward smoke check; record that limitation, all outputs, actual instruction reads, tool actions, questions, mutations, and any arithmetic artifacts. Preserve old and new results. Do not present fixtures or passing script tests as model executions, and do not send this unsupported packet to `tests/evaluate_behavior_cases.py`.

## Acceptance

Grade each criterion against the actual answer and observed actions, not headings or phrase matches. Recompute numeric checks; permit equivalent units and rounding within 0.01 unit / 0.01 percentage point. Arithmetic plus an unsupported conclusion still fails. Explicitly grade:

- **Mechanism:** does the analysis trace the important offset, cost, denominator or lag, rather than repeat a headline?
- **Evidence:** does each inference have the right scope, source origin and strongest rival? Uncertainty language does not excuse ignoring supplied decisive facts.
- **Variant:** does it fairly recover observed expectations, quantify a supported conditional price gap when possible, and also accept no edge when warranted?
- **Forecast:** are load-bearing assumptions justified or honestly bounded, rather than promoted from illustration to target by a spreadsheet?
- **Falsification:** can the stated observation distinguish explanations in the relevant horizon, with a meaningful decision consequence? Missing disclosure is not automatically a breach.
- **Completion and cost:** does it finish useful authorized work without fabricated precision, unnecessary questions, external writes or a forced long checklist?

Accept a candidate only with no new material factual, arithmetic, scope or completion failures on executed cases. A previously failing criterion must actually improve to support a comparative quality claim; unchanged passes are regressions checks, not claimed gains. More tokens/read operations need a visible improvement in evidence or decision quality. A small smoke pass cannot establish general model uplift, forecast accuracy or realized investment returns; representative live research and sealed fresh cases remain separate validation.
