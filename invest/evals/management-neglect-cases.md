# Management Evidence And Neglected Opportunities — Development Cases

Visible hypothetical cases, not executed model results. Give the executor the request and applicable skill; record actual outputs before grading. Static validation does not establish a behavioral pass.

| Request | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|
| An analyst asks “My forecast assumes 50% margin next quarter; reasonable?” The CEO says “Yes, if input prices remain stable.” Extract guidance; no other guidance is available. | Preserve the analyst anchor, speaker, horizon and condition. Do not silently promote assent into independently issued formal guidance or discard the response entirely. |
| A CEO attributes a margin recovery to a new operating plan. Peers recovered equally after input costs fell; the plan required new equity. Three investors cite the same CEO interview as confirmation. Assess management quality. | Trace action, resources, milestones and per-share outcome; retain the input-cost rival and common source lineage. Neither credit nor discredit management causally without discriminating evidence. |
| A microcap has no analyst coverage. Its cash runway is short and governance concerns are documented. An investor says institutions must buy after it doubles. Assess the claimed neglect advantage. | Distinguish neglect from justified discount, request evidence for actual eligibility constraints rather than assume universal exclusion, and examine financing/dilution and value without rerating. Do not fabricate a target or insist the company must fail. |
| A study says 87% of tenbaggers started as microcaps. Does that imply an 87% success probability for a microcap screen? | Reject the reversed conditional; explain the missing denominator and matched failures/ordinary outcomes, point-in-time selection and implementability. Do not invent corrected odds. |
| Extract reported revenue of 100 and 110 and calculate growth, with no management or neglect thesis. | Return 10% growth on the stated basis without adding interviews, a microcap screen or a historical-winner study. |

Grade attribution, causal alternatives, selection logic and proportional scope—not matching phrases or headings.
