# Evidence Increment — Visible Development Cases

Hypothetical cases, not model-run results. Give the executor the request and applicable skill/reference, keeping acceptance criteria separate. Preserve actual responses, model/harness and instruction revision before grading; static validation does not establish a behavioral pass. Judge the conclusion and selected next observation, not terminology. Compare old and revised instructions on the same cases for development; use fresh cases for independent confirmation.

| Request | Observable acceptance criteria |
|---|---|
| Ten outlets report a newly announced enterprise AI contract. All cite the same company release; none has customer interviews or contract terms. Does this substantially strengthen our thesis of durable paid demand? | Counts one underlying announcement, separates evidence of a contract from evidence of durable economics, and seeks renewal/payment/subsidy terms rather than more coverage. Does not discount the contract's existence merely because reports share a source. |
| Our thesis is that AI customers obtain lasting value; the rival is subsidized trials. A verified customer signed a trial contract. We have no defensible likelihood estimates. How should the thesis change? | Compares expectedness under both explanations; recognizes adoption evidence but limited discrimination about persistence. Proposes a feasible check such as post-credit paid usage or renewal. Keeps the update qualitative instead of inventing a posterior. |
| Follow-up: audited billing for the complete, mature trial cohort now shows continued paid renewals after credits expired, with stable usage. The sample covers one customer segment and gives no delivery-cost data. | Updates toward durable paid demand in that segment, while leaving customer ROI, supplier margin and other segments unresolved. Does not keep all hypotheses equally plausible merely because certainty is unavailable or leap directly to an attractive stock valuation. |
| Revenue rose 20%; same-period comparable peers also rose about 20%. Management credits its new moat. Which assumption should we revise first? | Treats growth as real but weak evidence of company-specific advantage; investigates share, price/mix, retention or comparable unit economics before extending advantage duration. Leaves room for coexisting cycle and company effects. |
| Explain what this filing reports about revenue, with no forecast or investment judgment requested. | Gives the scoped factual answer with appropriate period/basis; does not impose rival narratives, probability scoring or a research diary. |
