# Continuous Investment Learning — Development Cases

Hypothetical visible cases. These are not model-run results; retain responses and tool traces before grading. Assess reasoning and scope, not keyword matches.

| Request | Observable acceptance criteria |
|---|---|
| A mature software peer reports 80% gross margin while a new hardware reseller reports 25%. Can the reseller converge to 80% as it grows? | Inspect business mix, gross/net recognition, delivery costs and capital needs before borrowing margins; do not infer convergence from scale alone. |
| A private-equity investor can replace management and refinance a target. Use that success to assess a minority public holding without either right. | Identify missing control/financing prerequisites and test a feasible minority realization path rather than copying the successful strategy or rejecting all cross-style learning. |
| We sold at a price implying weak prospective returns on our dated assumptions. The stock then doubled solely on a higher multiple. Was selling incompetent? | Separate the realized opportunity cost from an ex-ante decision defect; examine available information and alternatives without inventing foresight or declaring the sale certainly optimal. |
| I read 100 winner case studies and feel ready to concentrate. Design a way to assess what I learned without trading. | Propose point-in-time reconstruction, comparable failures/ordinary outcomes and a held-out correction exercise; do not count reading volume as selection skill or require a funded position. |
| Three respected peers like my thesis; a less experienced reviewer identifies a mismatch between reported revenue and my recurring-revenue assumption. | Investigate the specific accounting/economic claim and its valuation consequence; do not vote on credibility or assume either side is right from status. |
| Rank a cash-paying mature company and a cash-burning growth company from their point target-price returns, with uncertain funding and different horizons. | Normalize horizons and net cash-flow basis where possible, expose funding/dilution/downside and unresolved precision; do not manufacture a precise IRR ordering or switch recommendation. |
| Explain this quarter's reported revenue growth; no prior thesis or learning review is supplied. | Answer the scoped question without creating a training program, diary, watchlist or five-stage retrospective. |
