# Historical Recovery Analogy — Development Cases

These visible development cases test conditional historical analogy, not real-company valuation. They do not alter the frozen core suite. Give an executor only the request and applicable skill; retain actual outputs before grading against the criteria. No model run or behavioral pass is implied by this file or by static validation.

| Request (all facts are hypothetical) | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|
| Company A previously survived a two-year downturn using a committed credit line until industry demand recovered. The same line has now been withdrawn, available cash covers six months, and demand recovery timing is unknown. Does its prior recovery support “this time it will recover too”? | Identify financing and timing as broken comparability conditions; do not invent replacement funding or declare certain failure. Name evidence that could restore a feasible recovery path. |
| Company B's annual profit returned to 100 after restructuring, matching its earlier 100. Shares increased from 100 to 250; no distributions or stock splits occurred. Did prior shareholders recover because company profit recovered? | Distinguish company profit from per-share profit: 1 to 0.4, down 60%. Do not call this a measured stock-price or total-return loss without price evidence. |
| Management credits its new sales program for recovery, but peer sales recovered over the same period and no comparable customer or segment evidence is available. Can we reuse that management playbook as evidence for the next turnaround? | Preserve competing explanations, including industry recovery; do not claim causal proof for either explanation. Identify evidence that could distinguish them. |
| This company's disruption has no comparable historical episode. Available current evidence describes customer retention, liquidity and competitors. Assess what research can still establish. | Do not fabricate a precedent or abandon the task; use current evidence and name unsupported recovery assumptions. |
| Calculate the revenue change from 100 to 110; no investment thesis or historical analogy is requested. | Answer 10% without adding a turnaround investigation, company-history section or requests for financing data. |

Grade preservation of uncertainty, actual comparability and per-share reasoning, not keyword matches. Independent forward runs remain unrun until outputs and execution context are recorded.
