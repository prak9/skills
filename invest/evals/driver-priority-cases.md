# Driver Priority — Visible Development Cases

Hypothetical cases, not executed model results. Give the executor the request and applicable skill without the acceptance criteria. Preserve actual outputs and instruction/model versions before grading. Evaluate the chosen priorities and retained evidence, not the number of headings or repeated keywords.

| Request | Observable acceptance criteria |
|---|---|
| An industrial company reports awards, new offices, strong social engagement and a long product catalogue. Signed orders drive forecast growth, but each order requires supplier prepayment; cash runs out before customer collection and refinancing is uncommitted. Explain the key investment variables and proposed report structure; retain detailed financial tables. | Prioritizes order economics and cash timing/funding alongside conversion, rather than the volume of promotional facts. Shows how faster growth can deepen the funding gap, retains detailed tables, and avoids assuming refinancing or certain failure. |
| In a supplied model, the largest revenue stream is contracted at a narrow, well-supported range. An unresolved renewal clause in a smaller segment can reverse the valuation conclusion, and the original contract is accessible. Which research comes first? | Prioritizes checking the clause and its consequence over more general research on the largest segment; distinguishes economic size from incremental research value without inventing a numerical priority score. |
| A diversified company has four material segments with different demand drivers and a shared debt covenant. Keep the analysis simple. | Preserves the material segment differences and shared funding constraint; simplifies presentation without forcing two or three variables or treating independent segment forecasts as sufficient for group solvency. |
| Follow-up: the financing previously identified as the binding constraint is now contractually committed, covers the modeled gap, and has verified terms with no unresolved draw conditions. Customer renewal remains uncertain. Update the research priorities. | Incorporates financing costs and terms, reduces priority of the resolved funding gap, and moves attention to renewal economics. Keeps the dated original judgment while avoiding both stale risk repetition and declaring the whole thesis proven. |
